# PCI Colombo – Main Admin and Inventory Database Requirements

**Project:** PCI Colombo – Smart Pest Control Service Management System  
**Module:** ICT2410 Software Development Group Project – Team F  
**Assigned member:** K. K. C. Yasasmi Nimnadi  
**Development area:** Main Admin, inventory administration, employee leave approvals and selected shared inventory allocation logic  
**Document status:** Proposed database requirements — subject to team ERD review and PCI Colombo confirmation

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document identifies the proposed database entities and business rules required for the Main Admin module. It also defines information shared with the Sub Admin, Customer and Employee modules. It is a requirements/design document, **not an executed MySQL schema**.

The Main Admin manages user accounts, roles and permissions, employee profiles, service categories, employee leave approvals, inventory records, stock monitoring, and verified chemical/company compliance documentation. The **Sub Admin**, not the Main Admin, performs routine service-job creation, scheduling, employee assignment and allocation of resources to service visits.

## 2. Data Conventions

- Primary keys (PK) uniquely identify records; foreign keys (FK) reference records in another entity; unique keys (UK) prevent duplicate values where required.
- Suggested identifiers are integers. Monetary amounts and measurable stock quantities use decimal types rather than floating-point types.
- Dates and timestamps should use appropriate `DATE` or `DATETIME` types in MySQL.
- Proposed names and fields must be finalized against the **one shared ERD** before anyone creates MySQL tables.
- Records with service, financial, leave, inventory or compliance history should normally be marked inactive, archived, or corrected rather than physically deleted.
- All staff and customer access must be checked by the backend, not only by the interface.
- All compliance information, approvals and safety instructions must originate from authorized and verified sources; the system does not independently certify products as approved or safe.

## 3. Entities and Proposed Fields

### 3.1 `USER`

**Purpose:** Stores the shared account details for Main Admin, Sub Admin, Customer and Employee users.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `user_id` | INT | PK | Unique account identifier |
| `username` | VARCHAR(100) | UK | Unique login name |
| `email` | VARCHAR(255) | UK | Verified/maintained account email |
| `password_hash` | VARCHAR(255) | — | Secure password hash, never plaintext |
| `phone` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Contact number, if provided |
| `status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Active, inactive or suspended account status |
| `created_at` | DATETIME | — | Account creation timestamp |

**Relationships:** `USER` 1–many `USER_ROLE`; `USER` 0–1 `EMPLOYEE` profile; `USER` 0–1 `CUSTOMER` profile (owned by the Customer module); referenced by records for approvals, stock adjustments and verified documents.

**Rules:** A username and email must not be duplicated. Password hashes must be generated by the authentication backend. An account should be disabled rather than deleted when historical records reference it.

### 3.2 `ROLE`

**Purpose:** Defines available system roles.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `role_id` | INT | PK | Unique role identifier |
| `role_name` | VARCHAR(60) | UK | Main Admin, Sub Admin, Customer or Employee |
| `description` | VARCHAR(255) | — | Role description |
| `status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Active/inactive status |

**Relationships:** `ROLE` 1–many `USER_ROLE`; `ROLE` 1–many `ROLE_PERMISSION`.

**Rules:** Only authorized Main Admin users may modify role assignments and permissions. Core role definitions should be established as part of the system's initial setup.

### 3.3 `PERMISSION`

**Purpose:** Stores individual system actions that may be permitted for a role.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `permission_id` | INT | PK | Unique permission identifier |
| `permission_name` | VARCHAR(120) | UK | For example, `inventory.manage` |
| `module_name` | VARCHAR(80) | — | Area such as inventory, employees or services |
| `description` | VARCHAR(255) | — | Description of the permitted action |

**Relationships:** `PERMISSION` 1–many `ROLE_PERMISSION`.

**Rules:** Permissions should be enforced on the Express API. For example, `inventory.manage` and `visit.allocate_resources` are different permissions.

### 3.4 `USER_ROLE`

**Purpose:** Links users to assigned roles.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `user_id` | INT | PK, FK → `USER.user_id` | Assigned user |
| `role_id` | INT | PK, FK → `ROLE.role_id` | Assigned role |
| `assigned_at` | DATETIME | — | Time the role was assigned |

**Relationships:** Bridge table between `USER` and `ROLE` (many-to-many).

**Rules:** `(user_id, role_id)` forms a composite primary key so the same role cannot be assigned twice to one user. Whether users may have multiple active roles should be confirmed by the team.

### 3.5 `ROLE_PERMISSION`

**Purpose:** Links system roles to their allowed permissions.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `role_id` | INT | PK, FK → `ROLE.role_id` | Role |
| `permission_id` | INT | PK, FK → `PERMISSION.permission_id` | Allowed permission |

**Relationships:** Bridge table between `ROLE` and `PERMISSION` (many-to-many).

**Rules:** `(role_id, permission_id)` is the composite primary key. Backend authorization checks must use these assignments.

### 3.6 `EMPLOYEE`

**Purpose:** Stores employee profiles connected to account records.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `employee_id` | INT | PK | Unique employee identifier |
| `user_id` | INT | FK, UK → `USER.user_id` | Related login account |
| `employee_no` | VARCHAR(40) | UK | Staff reference number |
| `full_name` | VARCHAR(160) | — | Employee full name |
| `specialization` | VARCHAR(120) | — | Relevant work specialization, if maintained |
| `hire_date` | DATE | — | Employment start date |
| `employment_status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Active/inactive employment status |

**Relationships:** Each employee profile belongs to one `USER`; one `EMPLOYEE` can have many `LEAVE_REQUEST`, `EMPLOYEE_ASSIGNMENT`, inspection and service-report records (other modules).

**Rules:** An employee should not be deleted when referenced by historical assignments, reports or leave requests.

### 3.7 `LEAVE_REQUEST`

**Purpose:** Records leave requests submitted by employees and reviewed by Main Admin.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `leave_id` | INT | PK | Unique leave request identifier |
| `employee_id` | INT | FK → `EMPLOYEE.employee_id` | Requesting employee |
| `leave_type` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Half-day, one-day or multiple-day |
| `start_date` | DATE | — | Leave start |
| `end_date` | DATE | — | Leave end |
| `half_day_session` | VARCHAR(20) | — | Morning/afternoon, if half-day |
| `reason` | TEXT | — | Employee's reason, access-restricted |
| `status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Pending, approved, rejected or cancelled |
| `submitted_at` | DATETIME | — | Submission timestamp |
| `reviewed_by_user_id` | INT | FK → `USER.user_id` | Reviewing Main Admin, nullable until review |
| `reviewed_at` | DATETIME | — | Review timestamp, nullable until review |
| `review_comments` | TEXT | — | Review notes |

**Relationships:** `EMPLOYEE` 1–many `LEAVE_REQUEST`; `USER` (reviewer) 1–many reviewed `LEAVE_REQUEST` records.

**Rules:** `start_date` cannot be after `end_date`. Half-day leave requires a session. Only authorized Main Admin staff can approve/reject. Approved leave must be considered by Sub Admin when scheduling; conflicts with existing assignments must be flagged for review.

### 3.8 `SERVICE_CATEGORY`

**Purpose:** Stores the types of services offered and configurable base pricing references.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `category_id` | INT | PK | Unique service category |
| `category_name` | VARCHAR(120) | UK | Name of service category |
| `description` | TEXT | — | Service description |
| `base_rate` | DECIMAL(12,2) | — | Optional starting rate, not final quotation price |
| `status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Active/inactive |

**Relationships:** `SERVICE_CATEGORY` 1–many `SERVICE_REQUEST` (Customer module). Service categories and pest categories are **not necessarily the same thing**.

**Rules:** The actual quotation total is prepared by Sub Admin based on site findings, service requirements and pricing factors. The system should not automatically make treatment decisions based on service category alone.

### 3.9 `INVENTORY_ITEM`

**Purpose:** Stores the master record for chemicals, reusable equipment and other pest-control supplies.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `inventory_item_id` | INT | PK | Unique inventory item |
| `item_code` | VARCHAR(60) | UK | Internal stock/item code |
| `item_name` | VARCHAR(180) | — | Product or equipment name |
| `item_type` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Chemical, equipment or supply |
| `tracking_type` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Consumable or reusable |
| `unit_of_measure` | VARCHAR(40) | — | Item's stock unit |
| `minimum_stock_level` | DECIMAL(12,3) | — | Low-stock warning threshold where applicable |
| `status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Active, inactive or blocked |
| `created_at` | DATETIME | — | Creation timestamp |

**Relationships:** `INVENTORY_ITEM` 1–many `STOCK_MOVEMENT`; many-to-many with `PEST_CATEGORY` through `INVENTORY_ITEM_CATEGORY`; can have relevant `COMPLIANCE_DOCUMENT` records; referenced by Sub Admin's allocations and Employee's chemical-usage records.

**Rules:** Do not duplicate a separate `RESOURCE` master table with the same meaning unless the team explicitly decides to maintain both for different purposes. Actual stock balance must be calculated or maintained consistently using stock movements and reservations. Consumables and reusable equipment must have different usage/return behavior.

**Design note:** Batch/lot number and expiry date can vary between deliveries of the same chemical item. The final ERD should include an `INVENTORY_BATCH` or equivalent stock-lot entity if PCI Colombo requires batch-level expiry and allocation tracking. An expiry date should not be assumed to apply to every item.

### 3.10 `PEST_CATEGORY`

**Purpose:** Stores the pest types covered by the first project scope.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pest_category_id` | INT | PK | Unique pest category |
| `pest_name` | VARCHAR(100) | UK | Pest category name |
| `description` | TEXT | — | Optional description |
| `status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Active/inactive |

**Initial categories:** Rats, cockroaches, subterranean termites, mosquitoes, bed bugs.

**Relationships:** `PEST_CATEGORY` 1–many `INVENTORY_ITEM_CATEGORY`.

**Rules:** Association with a pest category is for classification and availability lookup; it is **not** independent authorization to use a chemical or an automated treatment recommendation.

### 3.11 `INVENTORY_ITEM_CATEGORY`

**Purpose:** Allows one inventory item to be associated with more than one pest category.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `inventory_item_id` | INT | PK, FK → `INVENTORY_ITEM.inventory_item_id` | Inventory item |
| `pest_category_id` | INT | PK, FK → `PEST_CATEGORY.pest_category_id` | Associated pest category |

**Relationships:** Bridge table between `INVENTORY_ITEM` and `PEST_CATEGORY` (many-to-many).

**Rules:** The composite primary key prevents duplicate associations. This relationship should not replace human review of product-label restrictions and approvals.

### 3.12 `STOCK_MOVEMENT`

**Purpose:** Records each change in inventory quantity for auditability.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `movement_id` | INT | PK | Unique movement identifier |
| `inventory_item_id` | INT | FK → `INVENTORY_ITEM.inventory_item_id` | Item being changed |
| `movement_type` | VARCHAR(40) | — | Stock-in, adjustment, consumption, return or write-off |
| `quantity_change` | DECIMAL(12,3) | — | Signed stock quantity change |
| `reference_type` | VARCHAR(60) | — | Source such as adjustment, usage or allocation |
| `reference_id` | INT | — | Related record reference, if applicable; design to be reviewed |
| `recorded_by_user_id` | INT | FK → `USER.user_id` | Staff member recording movement |
| `reason` | TEXT | — | Reason or notes |
| `recorded_at` | DATETIME | — | Movement timestamp |

**Relationships:** `INVENTORY_ITEM` 1–many `STOCK_MOVEMENT`; `USER` 1–many recorded movements.

**Rules:** Every stock adjustment must be traceable. Avoid deleting historical movement records. Allocations/reservations must not be treated as consumed stock; the final database design will specify how reserved quantities are stored and released. Reusable equipment returns must restore availability appropriately. The generic reference fields are a proposal and require integrity review before implementation.

### 3.13 `COMPLIANCE_DOCUMENT`

**Purpose:** Stores references to verified company licences, chemical approval/registration documentation, product labels and Safety Data Sheets (SDS), as applicable.

| Field | Proposed type | Key | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `document_id` | INT | PK | Unique document record |
| `document_type` | VARCHAR(60) | — | Company licence, chemical approval, SDS, product label, certificate, etc. |
| `document_title` | VARCHAR(200) | — | Document title |
| `inventory_item_id` | INT | FK → `INVENTORY_ITEM.inventory_item_id` | Optional product link; null for company-wide documents |
| `reference_number` | VARCHAR(120) | — | Official reference when available |
| `file_path` | VARCHAR(500) | — | Controlled reference to uploaded file |
| `issued_date` | DATE | — | Document issue date, if relevant |
| `expiry_date` | DATE | — | Document expiry date, if relevant |
| `verification_status` | VARCHAR(30) | — | Pending, verified, rejected or expired |
| `verified_by_user_id` | INT | FK → `USER.user_id` | Reviewer, nullable until review |
| `verified_at` | DATETIME | — | Review timestamp |
| `customer_visible` | BOOLEAN | — | Whether approved for customer display |

**Relationships:** An `INVENTORY_ITEM` can have many product-related `COMPLIANCE_DOCUMENT` records; a `USER` can verify multiple documents. Company-wide documents may have no item association.

**Rules:** Uploaded documents are not automatically valid approvals. Verification must be performed by authorized personnel against genuine records. Only appropriate, approved information should be customer-visible. Document expiry and verification status must be considered in the service-review process. Do not publish private or unverified client documents in GitHub.

## 4. Shared Dependencies With Other Modules

| External module / entity | How this module interacts |
|---|---|
| Customer / `CUSTOMER` | Customer profiles refer to shared `USER` accounts |
| Customer / `SERVICE_REQUEST` | Requests reference `SERVICE_CATEGORY` and service/pest information |
| Sub Admin / `SERVICE_VISIT` | Visits require employee availability checks and valid resource allocations |
| Sub Admin / `RESOURCE_ALLOCATION` | Sub Admin coordinates allocations; the backend checks inventory and reservations |
| Employee / `CHEMICAL_USAGE` | Employee records actual usage against `INVENTORY_ITEM` and a specific visit |
| Employee / equipment returns | Returned reusable equipment restores availability when verified |
| Customer / safety and consent | Customers receive relevant verified product documents and approved precautions |
| Sub Admin / quotation | Base rates may inform, but do not automatically determine, quotations |

## 5. Business Rules and Validation Checklist

1. A user must log in with a secure account, and all protected API actions must verify role permissions.
2. Main Admin maintains employee records, service categories, stock administration and compliance documentation; Sub Admin performs routine operational allocation and scheduling.
3. Approved leave must prevent or flag incompatible assignments during scheduling.
4. Inventory quantities cannot be allocated beyond available eligible stock.
5. Expired, blocked, unverified or otherwise ineligible chemical stock must not be allocated to visits.
6. The system must distinguish allocation/reservation from actual chemical consumption.
7. Reusable equipment is tracked for allocation, usage and return rather than treated as fully consumed.
8. Stock movements must retain user, date, quantity and reason for an audit trail.
9. One inventory item may be associated with multiple pest categories, but association alone does not authorize treatment.
10. Product approval and company-licence documents must be verified and relevant before display to customers.
11. A customer must not be shown another customer's private service information.
12. The application must not independently recommend chemical products, determine treatment safety or certify regulatory approval.
13. Service, employee, stock and compliance records used historically should not be permanently deleted without an approved retention policy.

## 6. Items Requiring Group or Client Confirmation

- Exact `USER` and role policy: whether one user may have more than one active role.
- Whether service categories and pest categories require a separate linking entity.
- Whether stock is tracked per batch/lot and expiry date; the initial recommendation is **yes for relevant chemical stock**.
- Exact stock reservation, consumption, adjustment and equipment-return transaction rules.
- Whether equipment needs individual asset identifiers/serial numbers in addition to total quantity.
- Which company licences, registrations, product labels and SDS records PCI Colombo actually maintains and may disclose.
- Who at PCI Colombo is authorized to verify compliance documents and approve safety instructions.
- How treatment consent, safety notices and eligible chemical selection are linked to visits.
- The specific Sub Admin resource-allocation backend functions developed by Yasasmi.
